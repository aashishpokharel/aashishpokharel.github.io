**Abstract:** The present disclosure provides a method of extracting information from an image of a document in which the document image is properly aligned to be processed, the regions containing the desired information are detected and extracted from the document image, a text machine-learning model is performed in which the handwritten text in multiple languages may be extracted and stored, and a user may review, edit, and translate the extracted information to create a standardized digital format of the information contained in the document
